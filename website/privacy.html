Privacy Policy

Draft of November 22, 2026

My Church Bible Study turns a sermon into a week of Bible study. This page says what information the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the profile picture and account identifier they provide.
  • What you give the app to make a study. Sermon audio you record or upload, photos of notes or bulletins, text you paste, and YouTube links.
  • What the app makes for you. Your studies, which days you have finished, and events read from a bulletin.
  • What you post. Comments, prayer requests, testimonies, your profile and the friends you add.
  • Your settings. Your church's name and location, service times, study preferences and reminder time.
  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist on this website, your email address.

Where it goes

  • Our database. Account details, studies, progress, posts and settings are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider.
  • Google, for AI processing. Audio, photos, text and transcripts are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce the study or read the bulletin. Church search also goes through Google.
  • Sign-in providers. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that company confirms who you are.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Location

If you allow it, the app checks your location while it is open to tell whether you are at your church. That check happens on your phone. We do not keep a history of where you have been.

What other people can see

Your name and profile picture are visible to other people using the app. A study is visible to the people you share it with. Posts and comments are visible to the people they are shared with. Your email address is not shown to other users.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier, and use them only to sign you in and set up your profile. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Delete any study, event or post you created, in the app.
  • Delete your account and everything stored with it, in the app's settings or by writing to [support email to be added].
  • Turn off location, notifications, camera or microphone access in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to [support email to be added].
